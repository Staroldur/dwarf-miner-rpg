Restore a purchase

Open the main menu and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account that originally purchased the Full Game Unlock.

Saved progress

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. Deleting the app may remove saved game progress.

Full Game Unlock

The Full Game Unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription.
