Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Dwarf Miner RPG does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts, or third-party tracking.

Game progress, settings, and purchase-entitlement information are stored locally on the device.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store payment-card or billing information.

Contact

If you contact the developer by email, the information you voluntarily provide will be used only to respond to your message.
